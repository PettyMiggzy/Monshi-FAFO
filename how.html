❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

Do I need a wallet to play?
No. You can play every game without connecting. But connecting unlocks tier perks (score multipliers, extra lives) and lets you climb the global wallet leaderboard.
How do I install this as an app?
On iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share button, scroll to "Add to Home Screen". On Android: a popup will appear asking to install, or tap menu → "Install app". Works exactly like a native app.
Are scores cheat-proof?
For now scores are submitted client-side, so determined cheaters could fake them. We're tracking every submission and obvious outliers will be removed before any prize payouts. Real on-chain verification coming.
Where do my scores get stored?
Scores live in a shared Supabase database that everyone can read but only insert into. Your name and wallet (if connected) get attached. No personal data beyond that.
Why connect a wallet if scores work without?
3 reasons: 1. Holding $MONSHI multiplies your scores automatically (up to 4x). 2. Special games like Royal Roulette & Monshi Heist are token/NFT-gated. 3. Your wallet ranks across all games on the global leaderboard, where the real flexing happens.
What's the deal with the 🎴 NFT bonus?
If you own a Monshi NFT (only 23 holders exist), you get +1.0x score multiplier and +2 extra lives on top of your token tier. NFT also auto-unlocks WHALE MODE games. 3+ NFTs unlocks ROYAL MODE. Get one at OpenSea.
Can I win actual money?
During limited-time prize events, yes. The arcade isn't a casino with constant payouts. Watch for gold banner announcements. Day-to-day it's about leaderboard glory and badges.
Is this affiliated with the Monshi team?
It's a community build by KING PETTY for the $MONSHI ecosystem. Linked from the official monshi-fafo.vercel.app site. No tokens were given for it — built free for the trenches.
How does the referral system work?
Get your unique link at monshi-fafo.vercel.app/refer.html. When someone connects their wallet through your link, they're permanently tagged to you. Top recruiters at launch milestones will get $MONSHI airdrops, NFT mint priority, and tier upgrades.
My wallet won't connect / the network is wrong
The arcade uses Monad mainnet (Chain ID 143). When you click connect, MetaMask/Rabby/Phantom should auto-prompt to switch. If it fails, manually add Monad: RPC https://rpc.monad.xyz, Chain ID 143, Symbol MON.
Can I play on mobile?
Yes. All 27 games work on mobile with touch controls. Install as a PWA for the full app experience (no browser bars, splash screen, home icon).
