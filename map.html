🔒 PRIVACY GUARANTEE
Before anything is sent to our database, your wallet address is hashed with SHA-256 in your browser. We store the hash, not the address. There's no way to go from a dot on the map back to a public wallet.

Location is city-level only (rounded to ~10km), pulled from your IP via ipapi.co. You can pin yourself once per wallet — re-pinning just updates the timestamp.

Don't want to participate? Don't click the pin button. Reading is free, no data collected.
