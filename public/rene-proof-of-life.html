// 04QUESTIONS.

CAN I PAUSE THE GAME?
No. Real life doesn't pause. Your Rene's vitals decay 24/7 whether you're at work, on vacation, or asleep. That's the point. Holding $RENE means accepting responsibility.
WHAT IF MY RENE DIES?
It's permanent. The wallet gets a public death badge — visible everywhere in the ecosystem. You can adopt a new Rene after a 7-day grief penalty. The dead one stays in the records forever.
DO BURNS COUNT TOWARD ANYTHING ELSE?
Total burns reduce $RENE supply, period. Beyond that, your wallet's lifetime burn count is on-chain and visible. Top burners get pack ranks and visible badges. Lifetime stats persist even after pet death.
WHY WOULD I DO THIS?
Because if your bag is going to live or die based on your conviction, it might as well do it visibly, with a face, with a name. Either you keep showing up or you don't. The pet is the proof.
CAN I HAVE MORE THAN ONE?
One per wallet. Multi-wallet farming is allowed but obvious — the dashboard ranks per-wallet and per-cluster. You can also gift adoption seats to other wallets via burn.
IS THIS REAL OR A MOCK?
Right now: prototype. Mechanics are spec'd, art slots are placeholder. Live deployment depends on the Rene team's call. If you're seeing this — you're early.
