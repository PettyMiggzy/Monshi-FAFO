// SECTION 05 · QUESTIONS

QUESTIONS, SER?

CAN I JUST BUY MY WAY IN?
No. Holding $WOLF doesn't get you in the Pack. Somebody has to vouch you. Membership is social, not financial. You can hold a bag forever and still be outside the pack if no one bets their rep on you.
WHAT GETS MY REP DOCKED?
Selling more than 50% of your bag in 7 days, getting reported by 5+ pack members for bad-faith behavior, vouching in a wallet that turns out to be a sniper bot, or sniping at a launch on a Pad-launched token. Repeat offenses = SHEEP tier. Public.
CAN I GET KICKED?
Yes. If 10+ Alphas + Lone Alphas vote to remove a member, they're out. Their voucher chain takes a rep hit too. Removed members can re-apply after 30 days, but they need a vouch from an Alpha or higher (more expensive).
WHY VOUCH IF I PAY THE PRICE?
Because good vouches build rep faster than anything else. Vouch in a wallet that climbs to Alpha — you get rep multipliers from their progress. Lone Alphas in the current pack got there mostly by vouching wisely, not by holding longest.
WHAT DO LONE ALPHAS ACTUALLY GET?
Engraved homepage slot. Custom emoji badge visible across the ecosystem. Veto power on close pack votes. Free vouches forever. And bragging rights. The list of all Lone Alphas is permanent — wallets that hit it once stay marked even if they later drop in rank.
IS THIS LIVE?
Right now: prototype. Mechanics spec'd, member data is mock. Live deployment is the team's call. If you're seeing this, you're early.
