03 FAQ

// Real questions. Real answers.

What happens if the admins never fire?

If the wall doesn't fire within 90 days, your MON unlocks automatically. Withdraw anytime, any reason. No questions, no fees. The wall serves the pack — not the other way around.

Why MON deposits, not $RENE?

The vault buys $RENE on the dip — meaning we need MON sitting ready to deploy when the chart drops. Depositing $RENE would defeat the purpose: we need fresh buy pressure, not recycled tokens.

Who controls the trigger?

3 Pack admins on a multisig. 2 of 3 must sign to fire. Admin wallets are public, on-chain, doxxed within the community. Decisions are visible. No single point of failure.

What happens to my MON when the wall fires?

The vault swaps everything for $RENE on Atlantis in one big buy. You get your proportional share of the freshly-bought $RENE bag, locked for 7 days while the chart heals. Then withdraw at the new average.

Is there an audit?

Custom contract launching in stages. Initial pool capped at 5,000 MON until proven. Full audit funded by the team after traction. Use only what you're willing to risk during the early phase.

Can I deposit small amounts?

Minimum deposit is 0.5 MON to keep the gas math sane. No maximum during open phase. Cap is on the vault total, not per-wallet.
